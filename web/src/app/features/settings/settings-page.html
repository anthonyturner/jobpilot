Guardrails in force

  • The server only listens on this computer (127.0.0.1). Other websites can't read or change your data.
  • Job boards are reached only through official APIs or connectors, rate-limited, and never scraped.
  • Job descriptions are sanitised before display. Links open in a new tab without referrer data.
  • Every status change is written to an append-only activity log.
  • Tailored resumes only reorder your own resume content. Anything new Claude writes is checked against your resume, and unsupported claims are flagged.
  • Nothing is sent without your approval. Automatic submission is off by default, needs the company name typed in, is capped per day, and is attempted at most once.
